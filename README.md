## Instant Buy versus spot trading

The main choice for many users is whether to use the simple Buy flow or the spot market.

### Instant Buy

Instant Buy is designed for convenience. You choose the amount, receive a quote, and confirm. You do not need to understand the order book or decide whether to place a market or limit order.

The disadvantages are that:

- The quoted price may include a spread
- Payment-provider charges may apply
- The quote can change before confirmation
- You have less control over execution
- The quoted BTC amount may differ from a rough calculation based on the market price

For a first purchase, this simplicity may be worth more than the ability to fine-tune execution.

### Spot trading

Spot trading gives you more control. You select a BTC pair, such as an available BTC/USD or BTC/USDT market, then place a market or limit order.

A market order usually fills immediately against existing orders and is typically charged at the taker rate. A limit order may receive the maker rate if it rests on the order book, but a limit order that matches immediately can still be treated as a taker order. The execution result, not the label alone, determines the applicable fee.

Spot trading is more suitable when you care about:

- The maximum price you are willing to pay
- The exact amount of BTC purchased
- Order-book execution
- Trading fees
- Partial fills
- Timing and liquidity

It also creates more opportunities for user error. Entering the wrong price, selecting the wrong trading pair, or misunderstanding the order size can be more expensive than paying for a simple quoted purchase.

Crypto deposits do not generally incur a network fee charged to the receiving account, according to OKX. Withdrawals are different: the withdrawing party pays the withdrawal fee, and the amount can vary by asset, network, transaction conditions, and platform updates.

Before withdrawing Bitcoin, check:

- The selected network
- The minimum withdrawal amount
- The displayed withdrawal fee
- The destination address
- Whether the receiving wallet supports that network

Bitcoin transactions are not reversible after they are confirmed. A wrong address or incompatible network can turn a routine withdrawal into a very expensive lesson.
